# Brief: one chart for a report

The data here is **real**, unlike the other derivations. It is the set of
WebAIM Million figures already cited in the corpus (February 2026 scan of one
million home pages), taken from entries #1029, #710, #707, #1031 and #712.
The chart makes no claim beyond what those entries record.

## The job

A single figure for an accessibility report to a non-specialist client, on
one page, to be read in under ten seconds. The finding it has to land: low
contrast text is the most common detected failure on the web, by a wide
margin, and the client's own site has it.

## Audience

A business owner who does not read charts often. They need the finding, the
scale of it, and the source.

## The data (per cent of one million home pages with the failure)

| Failure | Per cent | Corpus entry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low-contrast text | 83.9 | #1029 |
| Missing alternative text on images | 53.1 | #710 |
| Missing form input labels | 51.0 | #707 |
| Empty buttons | 30.6 | #1031 |
| Missing document language | 13.5 | #712 |

Source as recorded in the corpus: WebAIM, The WebAIM Million (February 2026).

## Constraints

- Static HTML and inline SVG. No library.
- Single hue with emphasis on the one bar the finding is about; the rest
  neutral.
- A table view of the same figures on the page.
- Must read in greyscale and at 390px.
