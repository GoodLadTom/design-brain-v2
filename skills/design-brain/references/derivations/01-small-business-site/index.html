What we make

Three kinds of work. Kitchens are most of what we do, and the other two grow out of the same joinery.

Fitted kitchens

Frame-and-panel or flat doors, solid timber or painted, with drawers that run on the same runners twenty years from now. We design the layout with you, make the carcasses and doors in the workshop, and fit it ourselves.

  • Oak
  • Ash
  • Painted tulipwood

Built-in storage

Alcove cupboards, wardrobes, window seats and bookcases, made to the wall rather than to a catalogue size, so nothing is scribed in with filler.

One-off pieces

Tables, benches and desks, usually for a room we have already worked in. We take a few of these a year.

The joiners

Dan Mercer trained as a cabinetmaker and has run the workshop since 2012. Ellie Ross joined in 2016 and does most of the kitchen design and fitting. There is nobody else: the people you meet on the visit are the people who make and fit the work.

We work within about 25 miles of Whitstable, which keeps fitting days short and means we can come back if something needs adjusting.
