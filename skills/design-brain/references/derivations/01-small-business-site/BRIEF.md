# Brief: Harbourside Joinery

**This business is fictional.** It exists so the derivation can show a full
brief-to-page reasoning chain without using anyone's real company. Nothing on
the page is a claim about a real firm.

## The client

Harbourside Joinery, Whitstable, Kent. Two joiners, Dan Mercer and Ellie Ross,
trading since 2012. They make fitted kitchens, built-in storage (alcoves,
wardrobes, window seats, bookcases) and one-off pieces (tables, benches) in
oak, ash and painted tulipwood. They work within about 25 miles of the
workshop.

## The job of the page

One job: get a homeowner who is planning a kitchen or built-in storage to
request a site visit. A site visit is free, takes about an hour, and ends with
a written estimate within a week.

## Audience

Homeowners, roughly 35 to 65, in north Kent, who are comparing two or three
local firms. They have looked at a national fitted-kitchen chain and want
something made rather than assembled. They are cautious about cost and about
being sold to.

## Attributes (each with what it excludes)

- **Craft.** Excludes anything slick or corporate: no glass, no gradients, no
  stock photography of kitchens they did not make.
- **Trustworthy.** Excludes urgency, discounts, and any number that is not
  true. There are no reviews or statistics in the brief, so there are none on
  the page.
- **Calm.** Excludes motion, exclamation marks, and more than one call to
  action.

## Tone

Formal-to-casual midpoint, serious, respectful, matter-of-fact. Plain words.
They say "we" and use the joiners' names.
